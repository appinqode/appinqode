Colorette: Generate color palettes

Create stunning color palettes, extract colors from images, build tints & shades, create design system, design mesh gradients, and export ready-to-use CSV, CSS, Xcode .colorset assets and SwiftUI code. Everything is built into one fast, lightweight, native macOS application designed to help you create beautiful colors and integrate them directly into your projects.

Iconset Kit: App Icon Assets

Iconset Kit is a simple and efficient utility for Apple developers and designers who need to create App Icon asset catalogs for Xcode. Instead of manually resizing images and preparing dozens of icon variations, Iconset Kit generates all required app icon sizes from a single source image and exports a ready-to-use .appiconset folder. Simply select an image, preview the generated icons, and export the asset catalog directly into your project. Perfect for independent developers, students, hobbyists, and professional teams building apps for Apple's platforms.

Melobar: Menu bar music player

Simplified offline menu bar music player for macOS. Perfect for users who want a clean, distraction-free way to enjoy their music while working. Supported files .aac .aiff .m4a .flac .mp3 .ogg .wav

Mopperase: Storage data cleaner

With a clean, intuitive interface, Mopperase lets you explore, analyze, sort, and remove unnecessary Xcode generated files in just a click, helping you recover precious disk space and keep your development environment optimized.
